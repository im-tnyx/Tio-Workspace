4. **Low-1 (Sixty-Second Boundary Predicate Correction — `PRRT_kwDOVtCmZc656o5e`):** Corrected the interval predicate in `reserve_email_send` from `>=` to `created_at > (v_now - INTERVAL '60 seconds')`, ensuring requests at exactly $\ge 60.000$ seconds are accepted while requests strictly $< 60.000$ seconds are rejected, fully aligning with the owner-approved 60-second timer.
