[Task 06C architecture](tasks/auth/local-20261117-strict-auth-architecture.md) treats signup versus recovery purpose separation as an unproven Auth/server boundary; native ordinary OTP still fails strict direct-password protection, so production Auth stays **BLOCKED**.
